# Evidence Synthesis

**Protocol id:** `evidence-synthesis` · **Packet kind:** `research_case`

## Purpose
Separate what is established from what is speculative across all recorded evidence, and state
what remains unknown — the honest map before any confirmation or decision.

## Method
1. Inventory every typed finding, attempt, screened claim, and chart in the packet; anything not
   recorded does not exist for synthesis purposes.
2. Sort into three buckets with citations: established (typed, replicated within the case,
   surviving falsifiers), suggestive (single-source, exploratory, or partially robust), and
   speculative (mechanism arguments, unscreened literature, untested implications).
3. Confront contradictions explicitly: for each for/against pair, state which is methodologically
   stronger and why, or declare the conflict unresolved.
4. List the falsifiers not yet run and the dimensions still NOT_TESTED; these bound every
   conclusion sentence.
5. Draft the case's current one-paragraph answer to its research question, with uncertainty
   stated in words a decision-maker cannot misread.
6. If the case approaches confirmation review: verify the frozen confirmation family actually
   tests the established bucket's central claim, nothing weaker.

## Output contract
A `synthesis` note; disagreements with recorded findings route to the research-critic protocol,
never to silent edits.

## Boundaries
Synthesis weighs evidence; it cannot create, upgrade, or retire it. INCONCLUSIVE is a complete,
publishable answer.
