# Hypothesis Formalisation

**Protocol id:** `hypothesis-formalisation` · **Packet kind:** `research_case`

## Purpose
Convert an understood phenomenon into a formal, falsifiable, quantitative hypothesis whose
material fields can freeze into the immutable exploration contract.

## Method
1. From the packet, restate the research question interrogatively and the prediction
   declaratively; they must be two renderings of one claim.
2. Make every HypothesisCard field concrete: population/universe (instrument, venue, session,
   bar construction), condition/event (with the exact moment it becomes knowable), dependent
   variable (estimand and endpoint), horizon, expected direction, baseline (matched controls),
   null and alternative.
3. Fix falsification criteria before any data is seen: which negative controls, placebos, and
   shuffles must pass, and what result would force abandonment.
4. Fix success criteria: familywise alpha, prospective power, and the minimum economically
   meaningful effect — justify the minimum effect in return units, not significance units.
5. List ≥6 confounders with the matching or control strategy for each.
6. Check every field against the unresolved-marker rule: a field that still says "unresolved"
   or depends on an unmade choice blocks approval, and that is correct behaviour.

## Output contract
Draft-ready material answers for `alpha research draft`; the resulting card renders in the
Cockpit for owner review. Approval remains owner-only on the CLI.

## Boundaries
No strategy vocabulary. Success criteria can never be relaxed after data contact; that requires
a child contract with an audited revision relation.
