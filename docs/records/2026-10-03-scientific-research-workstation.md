- The research report preset places equity, drawdown, diagnostics and backend figures together without requiring a price snapshot. Matplotlib figures retain existing exports, metadata, static labeling and watermarks. Layout/maximize/document tabs remain unchanged.
- Visible project picker merges paginated real project/case inventories, reports partial failures, allows explicit no-project browsing, and clears version/run/snapshot while retaining the viewed market/archive. Native interval buttons from stored markets open an exact-source chooser; recorded-run prices never switch to a market archive.
- Async mounts release standalone Bokeh views/documents/global registry entries, including failed mounts. Registry disposers cannot delete a replacement endpoint. Cursor traffic remains imperative; data/options update separately. Auxiliary plots mount on first expansion and remain mounted after closing. Small panels retain a readable main-plot minimum height and scroll expanded auxiliary content; plotting surfaces are contained so they cannot intercept adjacent disclosure controls. Readout rows have fixed height to avoid changing plot geometry as cursors move. Bokeh native display LOD is disclosed and ends after interaction; recorded responses and tables are unchanged. Redundant line hover picking is replaced by exact UTC/value readouts. Size changes coalesce after an 80 ms quiet period, retaining canvas instances and ranges while avoiding repeated axis layout during splitter movement.
