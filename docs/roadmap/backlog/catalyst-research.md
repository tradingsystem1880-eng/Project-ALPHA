# Catalyst, expectations and fundamentals research (W15)

**Status:** Scoped
**Rough size:** L

## Problem / why

Research dated catalysts, expectations, macro and fundamental mechanisms with eligible historical
sources, publication delays, event-group holdouts and price-only controls. Only dated evidence and
literature seams exist; no qualified catalyst/event histories. SEC XBRL fundamentals were set aside
as "their own phase" (filing-date PIT parser and store).

## What "done" looks like

Qualified event, filing and news histories with publication-delay eligibility; event-group
holdouts; incremental predictive controls against price-only baselines; a filing-date
point-in-time fundamentals store.

## Dependencies

Qualified event/filing/news histories (data rights and timestamps); W10 controls and W13
composition.
