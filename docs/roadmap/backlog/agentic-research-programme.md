# Autonomous research agent programme

**Status:** Scoped
**Rough size:** XL

## Problem / why

The owner wants ALPHA to be able to take a vague trading question and have an AI team investigate
it, critique itself and report back within a budget. RD-Agent is a core engine of the project
(owner, 2026-11-14), and this programme is how its research would become self-directed. Only an
"advisory teammate" slice has shipped (PR #56); the six planned slices were never started, so the
plan was moved here on 2026-11-14 (triage card 15, KEEP as a backlog item).

## What "done" looks like

The six slices of the [archived plan](../../work/archive/2026-10/2026-10-02-agentic-research-capability-program/02-plan.md):
a reconciled baseline, discovery exports, typed investigation proposals, loss-episode diagnosis, a
budgeted lead and critic with receipts and a final report, then an evaluated first vertical slice
with independent review. No new authority over data, promotion or execution.

## Dependencies

The RD-Agent runtime (triage cards 7, 9 and C), the backlog items
[RD-Agent autonomous research](rdagent-autonomous-research.md) and
[multi-agent specialist swarm](multi-agent-specialist-swarm.md), which overlap with it.
