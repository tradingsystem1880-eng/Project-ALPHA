# Local vision model and hosted finance reasoning

**Status:** Idea
**Rough size:** M

## Problem / why

The Codex research runtime deferred "local Qwen vision and hosted finance reasoning per owner
direction; Codex owns reasoning and image input". The checklist keeps finance language/extraction
models as screening candidates only, gated on rights, host resources and measured benefit.

## What "done" looks like

One candidate is admitted only after a matched comparison against the Codex baseline shows a
quality gain or non-inferior quality with a material efficiency/privacy benefit; rights, exact
checkpoint and host qualification are recorded.

## Dependencies

Owner direction to revisit; the specialist adoption rule; host resource qualification.
